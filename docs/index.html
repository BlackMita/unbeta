Minecraft Java 1.20.1 · Fabric modpack

Unbeta

Removing what you won’t miss. Reinventing what remains.

Version 1 · Unreleased

PremiseUnfeaturesRefeatures CatalogueModsReleases

Premise

A minimalist, Golden-Era-ish Minecraft. Unbeta removes features you won’t miss, then carefully reinvents or adds new ones that bring real fun back to the gameplay loop. Its nuances are meant to be discovered by playing, not by reading a wiki. So this wiki hides the details until you ask.

The flaws of vanilla Minecraft cannot be fixed by adding things. They are fixed by removing things, giving what remains a purpose, and supporting it with well-thought-out additions that integrate with everything core to “the real Minecraft”.

Many features people mistake for fun are really countermeasures for bad design. “We need fast travel!” No. You need slow travel that isn’t boring.

Much of what Unbeta does is too abstract to list as discrete features. How the game and its mobs behave has been changed subtly, rather than bolting on content that sounds cool on paper but exhausts its novelty minutes after discovery. Unbeta is trying to fix your taste, worn down by contemporary Minecraft’s shallow updates.

Unfeatures

What’s gone, and why it’s better without it.

Golden Era cutoff

Most items, blocks and mobs added after the official release (September 2011) are gone.

Reveal the details
Unbeta Core gates vanilla 1.20.1 down to the Java Beta 1.8 feature set: about 860 blocks, 1,000 items, 89 entity types and 28 structures are removed, while content from other mods is never touched. A few later things were deliberately brought back (see Catalogue). Removed items no longer drop from mobs, and removed mobs never spawn.

Overworld only

No Nether. No End. Obsidian won’t make a portal.

Reveal the details
Both dimensions are gated: travel is blocked and anyone inside is evicted. Netherrack, Soul Sand, Ghasts and Zombie Pigmen are removed. Terrain is classic Beta 1.7.3-style generation (Moderner Beta), with deepslate and the Far Lands switched off, and bits of later ages mixed in.

Magic is fake

Enchanting is gone, and so is brewing.

Reveal the details
The enchanting and brewing systems are removed. Some enchantment-like effects survive, silently repurposed. For example, gold tools mine with silent Silk Touch and the gold sword loots like Looting III.

Stone untooled

Stone tools are removed. After Wood comes Iron.

Reveal the details
The tier ladder is Wood → Iron → Diamond. Wooden tools have half their usual durability (29 uses). Gold tools sit outside the ladder with their own tricks.

Stone barrier

Cave walls don’t yield to a wooden pickaxe, but any pickaxe can mine any ore.

Reveal the details
Stone needs an iron pickaxe or better; with anything less it reforms. Obsidian needs a diamond pickaxe. Any pickaxe can mine any ore block, which drops its ore and leaves Cobblestone behind. Diamond ore takes twice as long to mine.

Light tiers

You must light, and relight, torches that burn out.

Reveal the details
Torches and Jack o’Lanterns are placed unlit and burn out over time. Permanent light sources are hard-won, implicit tiers. Sun, lava and fire are the only natural light on the surface. See Light.

Recent apocalypse

Villagers are gone. Every village stands abandoned.

Reveal the details
Plains and desert villages still generate, empty. Their workstations no longer open. There is lore behind it.

Unaquatic

Update Aquatic swimming is gone. The Drowned especially.

Reveal the details
Swimming, breeding and vine-climbing are reverted to Beta behaviour. Aquatic mobs past Beta are removed, and the oceans are emptier, with no kelp or seagrass generating.

Refeatures

What’s new or reinvented. Spoiler-free until you reveal.

Railways

Abandoned railways bring minecarts back to a game about mining.

Reveal the details
Worldgen carves a surface rail network, plus an independent underground grid at three depths (y 40, 24 and 8), with slopes, booster rails and bridges that descend to land. Rare abandoned minecarts sit on flat cave floors. Rail recipes give 32 rails (16 powered). Minecarts are faster, with less friction and better climbing.

Creepers actually creep

They stalk instead of rushing.

Reveal the details
Creepers stalk you, plant Boom Spores nearby and flee, grinning once they have planted. A creeper notices a Jack o’Lantern disguise.

The undead don’t stay dead

Killing something isn’t always the end of it.

Reveal the details
Zombie deaths sully a chunk, and the dead rise from it again later. Skeletons collapse into a Bone Pile that reassembles. Zombie bites spread a slow infection that can turn animals, and you, into the risen. See Corruption.

Spiders for the arachnophobic

Spiders climb properly now. Sorry.

Reveal the details
Nyf’s Spiders lets them walk walls and ceilings and path around almost anything. Baby spiders (cave spiders, retextured) hatch from deaths and grow into adults after one to three days.

“Look at me. I’m the Warden now.”

Endermen are no longer a nuisance. They are a threat.

Reveal the details
Endermen are invulnerable to weapons and die instantly in sunlight. They aggro on proximity (24 blocks) rather than eye contact, are kept back by bright light (level 10+) and glow berries, and move faster in water. An angry one nearby makes your screen warp, more strongly as it closes in.

Unlike Like

The Like Like from The Legend of Zelda, because why not.

Reveal the details
An amphibious mob that swaps in for one in five squid. It grabs you, blinds you, and steals from you before spitting you out.

Lockey

Soft protection for chests against theft and griefing.

Reveal the details
See Lockey in the catalogue.

New strongholds

Six new stronghold types are planned: two underground, two above the clouds, two on the surface.

Reveal the details
Status: the classic stronghold is reworked (its End Gate is now a locked vault, and Unmasons tend it), and the first sky stronghold, the Skyhold, is built. The rest are work in progress.

If it’s lit, a player did it

You should never find lit objects in the wild.

Reveal the details
Worldgen, loot and recipes all produce unlit torches and lanterns. Furnaces must be ignited before fuel burns.

No sunrise victories

Mobs don’t burn up in the morning, though Endermen are one-shot by sunlight.

Reveal the details
Daylight burning is switched off for zombies and skeletons.

Successors to the lost dimensions

Spiritual successors to the removed Nether and End mobs.

Reveal the details
The Goldreath takes the Ghast’s place in the skies around Skyholds. More are planned.

Catalogue

Everything you can currently find in survival. Things that exist in the game files but are soft-locked (unobtainable) are left out.

Unbeta’s own content

Light

Unbeta Torch

unbeta-content:torch / lit_torchitemblocklight

Comes unlit. Light it, and it burns down.

Reveal the details
  • Light it from fire, a furnace, a lit torch or Jack o’Lantern, or burning blocks from the Burnt mod.
  • Burns out over time, with a burn bar; burned-out torches turn back into unlit ones.
  • Goes out underwater: held while you are fully submerged, placed in flowing water, or dropped into any water (with a hiss).
  • Hitting a mob with a lit torch has a 50% chance to set it on fire for 4 seconds.
  • Lights up your surroundings when held or dropped.
  • Replaces the vanilla torch everywhere (recipes, worldgen, loot). The vanilla one is renamed “Vanilla Torch”. Can also be crafted with charcoal.

Unbeta Jack o’Lantern

unbeta-content:jack_o_lanternitemblocklight

A lantern you can light, wear and hide behind.

Reveal the details
  • Shearing a pumpkin gives an unlit Jack o’Lantern (carved pumpkins are soft-locked).
  • Burns for 20 minutes (24,000 ticks), and is waterproof.
  • Wearing an unlit one makes passive mobs flee; creepers see through it.
  • Lights from fire or a furnace, like a torch.

Glowsand

unbeta-content:glowsanditemblocklight

A glowing block that falls like sand.

Reveal the details
  • Crafted from sand and glow berries.
  • Light level 15, and glows in your hand.
  • Smelts into glowstone.

Furnace ignition

mechaniclight

A furnace won’t burn fuel until you light it.

Reveal the details
  • Ignite it with flint and steel or a lit torch; it needs relighting after it burns out.
  • Any lava bucket is the exception: lava is the flame, so it lights the furnace by itself.
  • Iron and Copper lava buckets burn 20,000 ticks; a Wood lava bucket burns 3,200.
  • An empty Wood Bucket burns like a wooden tool (one item).

Buckets

Wood Bucket

unbeta-content:wood_bucketitembucket

Three planks in a V. It carries a little, and doesn’t carry it for long.

Reveal the details
  • Fills only from water or lava source blocks, and never takes the source.
  • Pours only a small puddle of flowing fluid that drains away. It can never create a source block.
  • Water poured on grass or crops waters them like bone meal.
  • Lava burns through it in 5 seconds: dropped, it spills a lava puddle with a snuff; in a chest, it simply vanishes.
  • Milks cows. Wood milk drinks like normal milk.
  • Burns as furnace fuel when empty.

Copper Bucket

unbeta-content:copper_bucketitembucket

Three copper ingots in a V. Almost an iron bucket.

Reveal the details
  • Takes and places real sources, exactly like iron.
  • Lava burns through it in 15 seconds: dropped, it leaves a real lava source; in a chest, it vanishes.
  • Copper milk leaves you with Poison I for 5 seconds.

Ice Bucket

unbeta-content:ice_bucketitembucket

Three snow blocks, or three ice, in a V. Whatever it holds, it freezes.

Reveal the details
  • Fills like a Wood Bucket (sources only, never taking them) and pours like one.
  • After 15 seconds (blue bar), water freezes into an Ice block and lava into Cobblestone, in your hand, on the ground or in a chest.
  • The Ice Lava Bucket glows like any lava bucket.
  • Milk drinks like normal milk, or freezes into Ice Cream.

Ice Cream

unbeta-content:ice_creamitemfood

One big scoop of vanilla.

Reveal the details
  • Food (4 hunger), always edible.
  • Gives Fire Resistance for 1 minute and puts out any fire on you.
  • The bucket is eaten too.

Iron buckets

minecraft:bucketitembucket

The vanilla buckets, renamed.

Reveal the details
Iron Bucket, Iron Water Bucket, Iron Lava Bucket and Iron Milk Bucket. They behave as in vanilla.

Copper

Copper

minecraft:copper_oreblockitem

Copper is back as a Beta-era metal.

Reveal the details
  • Copper ore generates in new chunks; any pickaxe mines it, for one raw copper each.
  • A gold pickaxe drops the ore block itself.
  • Copper never oxidises.
  • Copper blocks and lightning rods are craftable.

Copper Nugget and Copper Wire

unbeta-content:copper_nugget / copper_wireitem

Small change and fine wire.

Reveal the details
  • One ingot makes nine nuggets, and nine nuggets make an ingot.
  • Three nuggets in a diagonal make copper wire.

Storage and security

Lockey

unbeta-content:lockeyitemchest

A key that binds to one chest.

Reveal the details
  • Crafted from an iron nugget and a gold nugget.
  • Right-click a chest with an unbound Lockey to lock it; the key changes to show it’s bound. Use the same key to unlock.
  • A locked chest can’t be opened, emptied by hoppers, moved by pistons or blown up. It tells would-be thieves where the key is.
  • Breaking a locked chest forces out one random item per swing, weighted by stack size, so burying valuables under junk works.
  • Right-click the air with a bound key to see where its chest is. Keys track chests carried with Carry On, and even locked mimics.
  • A Lockey never despawns and survives fire and explosions; only lava destroys it. A key whose chest is gone salvages back into a gold nugget.

Burnt Chest

unbeta-content:burnt_chestblockchest

A charred chest. You can only make one by smelting a chest.

Reveal the details
  • A normal 27-slot chest that pairs into a double with another Burnt Chest.
  • Breaking it drops only its contents; mine it with a gold pickaxe or axe to keep the chest.
  • Every stronghold vault is a locked Burnt Chest.

Clambox and Pearl

unbeta-content:clambox / unbeta_pearlblockitem

A sea-floor box that turns its contents into a throwable pearl.

Reveal the details
  • A two-slot block that falls like sand; found on the ocean floor with loot.
  • Left on a solid floor with a water source above, it slowly pearls its contents.
  • Throw a Pearl to release whatever is inside where it lands, even a lava bucket.

Obsidian Door

unbeta-content:obsidian_doorblock

A floating, glowing door that only opens for the experienced.

Reveal the details
  • Needs 550 experience points to open; it returns experience orbs when it closes or breaks.
  • As tough as obsidian, and drops itself.

Other items, blocks and mechanics

Boom Spore

unbeta-content:boom_sporeitem

A throwable grenade. Creepers grow them.

Reveal the details
  • Silent 6-second fuse, with a bright red bar.
  • Explodes on impact, on landing, in your inventory or in a chest, so don’t hold onto it.

Obsidian Fire

unbeta-content:obsidian_fireblock

What happens when you light obsidian.

Reveal the details
  • Lighting obsidian gives permanent, silent, smokeless fire in a random colour (red, yellow, green, blue or purple).
  • Mining the obsidian snuffs it out.

Seared Flesh

unbeta-content:seared_fleshitemfood

Meat from something that died burning.

Reveal the details
  • Dropped by the undead and corrupted animals that die on fire, and made by smelting.
  • Burning deaths are never remembered by sullied chunks.

Gold tools

minecraft:golden_pickaxeitemmechanic

Gold is soft, but special.

Reveal the details
  • Gold tools mine with a silent Silk Touch.
  • The gold sword loots like Looting III and makes zombies drop their gear.
  • Killing the undead with gold leaves nothing behind for a sullied chunk to remember.
  • Gold armour freezes the zombification countdown and blocks infected bites.

Golden Apple

minecraft:golden_appleitemfood

The cure.

Reveal the details
  • Cures Inevitable Zombification and hidden bites.
  • Feeding one to a corrupted animal cures it instantly.
  • Raises maximum health through the Attrition datapack.

Name Tag

minecraft:name_tagitem

No anvil needed.

Reveal the details
  • Craft from any nugget and paper.
  • Write the name, then right-click a mob.

Beta names for stone

minecraft:graniteblock

Granite, Diorite and Andesite are gone from worldgen.

Reveal the details
They are retextured as stone and renamed Anstone, Stonerite and Stonite.

Small recipe changes

recipe

Old materials, new uses.

Reveal the details
  • Rotten flesh smelts into leather.
  • Pigs follow apples; chickens follow seeds.
  • Glow berries are back as a light source and ingredient.

Mobs

Zombie

minecraft:zombiemobundead

Slow to start. Not slow for long.

Reveal the details
  • Speeds up the longer it chases you, up to double speed after about two minutes; resets when it hits or is hit.
  • Hunts the nearest prey, players and livestock alike, and its bites can carry infection.
  • Doesn’t burn in daylight. Its death sullies the chunk.

Revenant

unbeta-content:revenantmobundead

What rises when you lose to the infection.

Reveal the details
  • Dying while infected raises a zombie wearing your armour, holding your items and carrying your whole inventory.
  • It never despawns, even in Peaceful, never drowns and picks nothing up.
  • Killing it returns everything.

Zombie Cow, Pig, Sheep and Chicken

unbeta-content:zombie_cow …mobundead

Bitten livestock, turned.

Reveal the details
  • Green-tinted, hostile, with zombie voices; zombie sheep keep their wool colour.
  • They speed up while chasing (a cow’s charge knocks you back hard).
  • A golden apple cures them instantly, name and colour kept.

Skeleton

minecraft:skeletonmobundead

Knocking one down doesn’t keep it down.

Reveal the details
  • Dies into a Bone Pile that reassembles after 20 seconds, keeping its items.
  • One in six spawns with a damaged wooden sword; one holding junk is harmless.
  • No daylight burning, no shade seeking.

Creeper

minecraft:creepermob

It creeps.

Reveal the details
Stalks, plants Boom Spores and flees. It grins after planting.

Enderman

minecraft:endermanmob

The Warden now.

Reveal the details
Invulnerable to weapons, killed by sunlight, aggroed by proximity, held back by light and glow berries. Navy particles and eyes.

Spiders

minecraft:spidermob

They climb walls and ceilings.

Reveal the details
  • One in eight natural spawns is a baby (a cave spider with 2 health) that grows up in one to three days.
  • One in eight deaths bursts into babies.
  • No poison.

Unmason

unbeta-content:unmasonmob

The stronghold’s silent caretaker.

Reveal the details
  • Rarely replaces a zombie, more often the closer you are to a stronghold, which it drifts toward.
  • Inside, it freezes under your gaze and follows whoever last looked at it.
  • Repairs damage to the stronghold’s masonry and bricks over doors, rising from the floor in crews when needed.
  • Gives off a Mining Fatigue II aura. Never spawns inside a Skyhold.

Unlike Like

unbeta-content:unlike_likemob

It wants your stuff.

Reveal the details
  • Replaces one in five squid; at home in water and on land.
  • Grabs, blinds and robs you, then spits you out.

Goldreath

unbeta-content:goldreathmob

A golden, winged ghost of the skies.

Reveal the details
  • Near a Skyhold it is hostile: it fires homing bolts that give strong Levitation, and calls lightning on anyone beneath it.
  • A well-timed swing destroys a bolt.
  • Far from Skyholds, a rare one is a silent guide that drifts toward the nearest Skyhold while you follow. Hit it and it flees skyward.
  • Glows like glowstone. Drops a Golden Feather (Cloud Boots).

Mimic

bettermimic:mimicmob

Not every chest is a chest.

Reveal the details
  • One in eight generated loot chests is a mimic in disguise. They never spawn any other way.
  • Aggressive in the dark, passive in bright light.
  • A Lockey can lock a disguised mimic shut; it then behaves like a locked chest.

Corruption

mechanicundead

Death leaves a mark on the land.

Reveal the details
  • Sullied chunks remember up to 16 undead and bitten deaths, and raise them again over time (always at night, rarely by day), out of the ground.
  • Burning deaths and gold kills are never remembered; scorched ground stops the dead rising nearby.
  • Sullied ground puffs green spores and tints your view.
  • Bites from carriers infect players (1 in 4, blocked by gold armour) and livestock.
  • Rotten flesh can infect you too.

unbeta-content:inevitable_zombificationeffect

A slow death sentence, with one cure.

Reveal the details

  • Gold armour freezes the countdown. Milk and totems do nothing.
  • A golden apple cures it.

Structures

Stronghold

minecraft:strongholdstructure

The classic stronghold, tended and locked.

Reveal the details
  • The End Gate is sealed and topped with a locked Burnt Chest vault holding a Hookshot; its key is hidden in one of the stronghold’s other chests (or inside a mimic).
  • Unmasons repair any damage you do.

Skyhold

unbeta-content:skyholdstructure

A stronghold hollowed into floating islands, high above the clouds.

Reveal the details
  • A broad main island ringed by dozens of smaller ones, between y 200 and 300, wrapped in thick fog.
  • Brick-and-copper workshops inside, with towers and chimneys breaking through the grass, and ruins on every island.
  • Its End Gate room holds a locked vault with Cloud Boots.
  • Goldreaths haunt the air around it; their bolts are the way up.

Highlights

  • A Beta 1.8 world on modern Minecraft: Overworld only, no enchanting, no stone tools.
  • Light you have to keep alive: burning torches, lanterns, ignited furnaces.
  • The dead return: sullied chunks, bone piles, a creeping infection.
  • Two kinds of stronghold to raid, each with a locked vault.
  • Railways, Lockeys, mimics, and a few new monsters.

Added

  • Rule engine and content gates (Beta 1.8 baseline).
  • Unbeta Torch, Unbeta Jack o’Lantern, Glowsand, furnace ignition.
  • Boom Spore, Bone Pile, Seared Flesh, Obsidian Fire, Obsidian Door.
  • Lockey, Clambox and Pearl, Burnt Chest.
  • Wood, Copper and Ice Buckets, Ice Cream, copper nuggets and wire.
  • Unmason, Unlike Like, Revenant, corrupted livestock, Goldreath.
  • Inevitable Zombification and the corruption system.
  • Surface and underground rail networks, cave minecarts.
  • Stronghold vault; the Skyhold.
  • Better Mimic, Carry On, Cloud Boots and Hookshot integrations.
  • Custom title screen.

Changed

  • Tool tiers: Wood → Iron → Diamond; stone barrier; ores leave cobblestone.
  • Creepers, Endermen, Zombies, Skeletons and Spiders reworked.
  • No daylight burning for hostile mobs; fire damage doubled.
  • Minecart physics and rail recipes buffed.
  • Iron buckets renamed; Cyan Hookshot renamed Hookshot with built-in Range.

Removed

  • The Nether and the End.
  • Enchanting and brewing.
  • Stone tools.
  • Villagers.
  • Modern swimming and breeding.
  • Roughly 1,900 post-Beta blocks and items, and 89 entity types.
